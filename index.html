About Dr. Bhattacharya

The mathematician behind the mathematics of uncertainty.

0 YEARS IN
RESEARCH

National Post-Doctoral Fellow @ Indian Statistical Institute

Current AffiliationIndian Statistical Institute, Kolkata
UnitMachine Intelligence Unit (MIU)

Anushree is a National Post Doctoral Fellow (N-PDF, ANRF, Govt. of India) at the Machine Intelligence Unit, Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata. She was previously a research fellow on a project funded by the West Bengal Department of Higher Education, Science & Technology and Biotechnology (WBDHESTBT).

She holds a Ph.D. and an M.Sc. (Gold Medalist in Mathematics) from Vidyasagar University. Her research centers on fuzzy graph theory, fuzzy optimization, algorithmic techniques, and decision-making under uncertainty — applied to economics, healthcare, sustainability, and infrastructure.

She is currently also an Honorary Visiting Fellow at the Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology, University of Technology Sydney, Australia.
